Ethical Dairy Farming

Our Buffalo Breeds & Welfare Standards

Healthy, happy animals yield superior quality milk. We treat our livestock like family.

Murrah Buffalo Breed

Originating from Punjab and Haryana, Murrah buffaloes are world-renowned for their high milk yield and dense fat concentration (7.5% - 9%). They are recognized by their tightly curled horns and deep black coat.

  • High Natural Calcium & A2 Beta-casein Profile
  • Superior taste and thick cream formulation
  • Robust immunity against tropical climate illnesses

Jaffarabadi Breed

Known for their massive frame and high butterfat yield, our Jaffarabadi stock complements our herd by giving exceptional consistency for ghee and sweet production.

  • Rich aroma in churned butter
  • Naturally sweet milk profile
  • Gentle temperament and high resilience
